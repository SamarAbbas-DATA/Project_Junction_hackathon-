# Dashboard partner logos

The TYKS and MYLAB artwork matches the company logos supplied for this project. Assets are served locally from `apps/web/public/branding`, retain their original colours and proportions, and are displayed in the navigation sidebar and footer by `PartnerLogos.tsx`.

Original asset locations:

- MYLAB vector wordmark: https://mylabgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Logo.svg
- TYKS transparent PNG: https://mylabgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Logo_RGB_TYKS-1.png

The artwork belongs to its respective company. It is used for the requested project branding, separately from the AMR Hub application name.
